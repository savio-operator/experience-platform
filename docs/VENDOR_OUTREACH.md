Subject: Kochi experience pilot — service and booking terms enquiry

Hello,

We are preparing a small, ops-managed Kochi experience pilot and would like to discuss
whether you would accept bookings referred or arranged by us. We are not currently
claiming a partnership or listing your inventory as available.

Could you confirm:

1. Your trading/legal name, official booking contact and person authorized to agree terms.
2. The specific experience offered, exact meeting point, duration, inclusions/exclusions,
   minimum/maximum participants and booking lead time.
3. A written current quote, its validity, any applicable taxes/fees, payment timing and
   whether we may refer or arrange bookings. Please distinguish customer price from
   any partner rate; no commission arrangement is assumed.
4. How you confirm dates and capacity, whether you can hold inventory, the hold deadline,
   release process, and how changes/unavailability are communicated.
5. Cancellation, rescheduling, weather and provider-failure terms, and the responsible
   contact for a problem on the day. Please provide your actual terms.
6. Relevant accessibility and participation restrictions. For cooking: ingredients,
   dietary/allergy handling and whether requirements can be reliably accommodated.
   For kayaking: safety briefing/equipment, guide qualifications, operating conditions,
   emergency process and applicable operating permissions, with supporting evidence.
7. Written permission, if agreed, for how we describe/use your name, material and service,
   and any booking/referral conditions we must follow.

We would confirm any customer booking with you explicitly before representing it as
confirmed. Please identify any information that must remain private.

Thank you.
